# Frontend design

Visual thesis: a financial workspace with black surfaces, white actions, pale blue highlights, and the Satoshi typeface from bevor.io.

Content plan: show the agreement register first, then the selected vesting schedule, claim conversation, and fixed audit terms.
The final action follows the connected wallet role and current contract state.

Interaction thesis: agreement selection reveals its detail pane, a moving schedule marker shows elapsed time, and dialogs guide reviewed submissions.
Reduced-motion preferences disable entrance and progress animations.

The logo and Satoshi font come from the Bevor website.
The header and browser icon use the supplied Bevor logo.
